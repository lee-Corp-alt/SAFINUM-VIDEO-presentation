ABOUT THIS DEMO

LexisFlow is a Chrome extension that sits on top of an existing banking page. It shows the text in Ewondo, Fulfulde or Duala, reads any part aloud with a voice that runs on your own computer, and never changes the page’s code.

Key takeaways

Amounts, fees and phone numbers are locked before translation and checked after. Nothing leaves the browser. The Ewondo, Fulfulde and Duala texts are drafts awaiting native-speaker review.
